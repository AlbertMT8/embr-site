Round 2 — depth

2a Orbit separates the layers straight along their own normal while the camera swings 54 degrees around the stack. Nothing drifts diagonally, the dimensionality comes from the move itself.
2b Slab grows a top and side wall as the layers part, so the stack reads as one solid block of data being sliced rather than four loose sheets. Most literally 3D.
2c Time corridor is the 4D one. The planes are not modalities, they are consecutive frames, and you look straight down the tunnel of a movement in progress. Layer content still overlays on the front frame.
2d Helix winds each layer out around a vertical axis and up, so the stack unspools into a spiral you can read from the side.

Notes

Every option uses the same four layers and the same scroll driver, so whichever you pick drops straight into Coverage and Access. Scrub again to compare, then tell me an id.

1a Peel keeps the raw frame planted and swings the derived layers open on a hinge. Most physical, reads as pulling signal off the footage.
1b Cascade drops each layer straight down with no lateral drift. Calmest, closest to a spec sheet.
1c Quad abandons depth entirely and splits into four coplanar panes. Shows all four modalities at full size at once.
1d Shutter wipes the single frame into vertical bands. Strongest idea, one moment of work seen four ways, and nothing like Scale.
